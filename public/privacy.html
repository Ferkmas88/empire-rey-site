Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

🇺🇸 ENGLISH

EMPIRE REY AUTO SALES #2 LLC ("we", "us"), operating empireautorey.com, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, phone, preferred vehicle, trade-in details, and your message.
  • Test Drive Requests: Full name, driver's license info (collected in person only, never through the website), phone, preferred date.
  • Financing Pre-Qualification: Employment info and income range (only if you request financing).
  • Technical Data: Browser, IP, device type, pages viewed (collected automatically for security).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries about vehicles and repair services
  • Schedule test drives and service appointments
  • Process vehicle sales, trade-ins, and financing applications
  • Comply with Kentucky and federal motor vehicle laws
  • Meet the FTC Safeguards Rule requirements for auto dealers

3. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Lenders and financial institutions when you apply for financing (with your consent)
  • The Kentucky Department of Motor Vehicles for title and registration
  • Insurance companies when required to complete a sale
  • Law enforcement when required by law
  • Service providers (hosting, email)

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookies Policy for details about what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

5. Data Retention

  • Contact form submissions: up to 2 years
  • Sales records, titles, bills of sale: 5+ years (Kentucky dealer law)
  • Service records: 3 years
  • Financing applications: per federal requirements

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data (subject to legal retention).

7. Security (FTC Safeguards Rule)

As a licensed motor vehicle dealer, we maintain a written information security program including access controls, HTTPS encryption, employee training, and incident response procedures, as required by the FTC Safeguards Rule.

8. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13.

1. Información que Recopilamos

NO vendemos tu información personal. Solo compartimos datos con:

Como concesionario licenciado, mantenemos un programa escrito de seguridad de información que incluye controles de acceso, cifrado HTTPS, capacitación de empleados y procedimientos de respuesta a incidentes, según lo requiere la FTC Safeguards Rule.

8. Privacidad de Menores

Este sitio no está dirigido a menores de 13 años.
